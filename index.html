Common questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Our service is free for employers. You pay your students the wage you agree on, like any other seasonal staff.

Is Sapar Staffing a J-1 sponsor?

No. Visa sponsorship is provided by CIEE, a U.S. Department of State–designated sponsor. We find and prepare the students, connect them with you and help both sides through the sponsor's process.

When should we start?

As early as possible. The strongest students usually secure their job offers by winter, so November to February is the best time to reserve your team.

What if a student leaves early or isn't a good fit?

Tell us right away. We talk to the student, work with CIEE on the next steps and, whenever possible, help you find another student to fill the spot.

Is there a minimum number of students?

No. You can hire just one student or a whole team.

We haven't hired J-1 students before. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We walk you through each step of the sponsor's employer process and answer questions along the way.
