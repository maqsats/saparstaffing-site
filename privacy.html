Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Sapar Staffing ("we", "us") collects through saparstaffing.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email us at saparali@saparstaffing.com.

Who we are

Sapar Staffing is based in Almaty, Kazakhstan. We help businesses in the United States find university students from Kazakhstan for seasonal jobs through the J-1 Summer Work Travel program. We are not a J-1 visa sponsor; sponsorship is provided by designated U.S. sponsors such as CIEE.

Information we collect

When you fill out our employer request form, we collect the information you enter, such as:

  • business name, website, type of business and business address;
  • contact person's name, job title, email address and phone number;
  • job details: positions, dates, pay, hours, job duties, tips and overtime;
  • housing details, including housing cost and address, if you provide them.

We also receive the information you send us by email or WhatsApp. Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use your information

  • to reply to your request and discuss your staffing needs;
  • to match your positions with suitable students and arrange interviews;
  • to prepare job offer paperwork for the J-1 sponsor;
  • to keep in touch with you before and during the season.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Students we propose to you. We share job details (positions, dates, pay, duties, housing and your business name and location) so they can decide whether to apply.
  • The J-1 sponsor (for example, CIEE), when you decide to hire students and the job offer is submitted.
  • Service providers that help us run the website and email, such as our website host (GitHub Pages), the form service that delivers the request form to our inbox (FormSubmit) and our email provider (Google Workspace). They process data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored with our service providers, which may keep it on servers in the United States and other countries. We take reasonable steps to keep it secure, such as limiting access to our team.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we work together, and for up to 3 years after your last season with us, so we can help you again in future seasons. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email saparali@saparstaffing.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Third-party content

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. Links to other websites are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
